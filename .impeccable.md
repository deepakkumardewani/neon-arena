# NeonArena — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

Two overlapping audiences:

1. **Casual mobile players** (students, people killing time) — want instant gratification, no friction, feel cool doing it. Phone, portrait, anytime.
2. **Company/team players** — organised internal matches, slightly older, likely desktop. Want something they're proud to share with colleagues.

Both share one trait: they will judge the product by its first impression. The UI _is_ the product hook.

### Brand Personality

**Aggressive. Electric. Nostalgic.**

- Aggressive: confident, no apologies, sharp edges, direct hierarchy
- Electric: live, pulsing, high-energy — feels like something is always happening
- Nostalgic: nods to 80s/90s arcade culture — not retro kitsch, but the _feeling_ of a cabinet in a dark arcade

Emotional goal: players should feel **hyped and ready to battle** the moment the home screen loads. Like feeding a coin into an arcade machine.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme:** Dark — near-black backgrounds. This is played at night, on phones, in competitive headspace. Dark is correct.
- **Color:** Keep brand hues — Cyan (`#00F5FF`) for X, Rose (`#FF2D78`) for O, Purple (`#7B61FF`) for structure. Migrate to OKLCH for all token definitions. Tint neutrals with a whisper of purple toward the brand.
- **Anti-references:** No glowing cyan-on-dark "AI dashboard" aesthetic. No glassmorphism. No gradient text. No generic "gaming" purple/blue gradient backgrounds. No rounded cards with drop shadows repeated in a grid.
- **References (feeling, not visuals):** The energy of a Tron arcade cabinet. The typographic confidence of a 1980s Japanese arcade flyer. The restraint of a high-end dark UI that knows exactly what it's doing.

### Typography

Brand voice words: **aggressive**, **electric**, **nostalgic**

Reflex fonts to reject (per impeccable guidelines): Inter (currently used as body — must replace), Orbitron (already in use for headings — evaluate keeping vs. replacing).

**Orbitron assessment:** It's a strong fit for "electric + nostalgic arcade" and is already part of the spec's brand identity with glow token names tied to it. Keep Orbitron for display/headings — it earns its place here.

**Body font:** Replace Inter. For "aggressive + electric + nostalgic" body copy, look toward:

- Something with a slightly mechanical or condensed character
- NOT a serif (wrong energy), NOT another geometric sans that becomes Inter 2.0
- Candidates to evaluate: Barlow, Rajdhani, Exo 2, Chakra Petch, Noto Sans Display (condensed weights)
- **Chosen:** `Chakra Petch` — Thai-influenced geometric sans, slightly mechanical, available on Google Fonts, unusual enough to feel designed, fits the arcade/cyberpunk energy without being cliché

### Design Principles

1. **First impression is the product.** The home screen must be the kind of thing someone screenshots and sends to a friend. No generic game portal energy.
2. **Energy through restraint.** The glow effects, particles, and animations work _because_ the rest of the layout is disciplined. Don't animate everything.
3. **Dark done deliberately.** Not "dark because cool" but "dark because that's when and how people play this." Every surface, tint, and border shade serves the theme.
4. **Asymmetry over symmetry.** Left-aligned, varied spacing, intentional grid breaks. Centered-everything feels like a template.
5. **Typography carries the brand.** Orbitron for anything score/title/heading. Chakra Petch for everything else. Never a third font family.
